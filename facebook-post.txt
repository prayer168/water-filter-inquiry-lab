💧 自製濾水器探究｜清澈，不等於安全

一瓶泥水、一組砂石，可以問出多少科學問題？這套適合國小高年級至國中七年級的互動教材，不直接給學生「最佳排列」，而是帶他們觀察沉澱、篩濾與吸附，自己設計公平實驗，比較清澈度、流速、出水量與成本，再用證據改良濾柱。

教材包含十種濾材圖鑑、可調整流向與厚度的配置模擬器、三次重複實驗紀錄、十題素養挑戰，以及自來水廠、雨水回用、咖啡、魚缸、露營與太空站等六則 Image 2.0 原創生活情境圖。

最重要的安全觀念也放在每個學習環節：所有模擬水樣過濾前後都不可飲用；水看起來變清澈，只是觀察結果，不代表通過飲用水檢驗。歡迎帶學生一起從「看起來」走向「有證據」。

【English】
💧 Water Filter Inquiry Lab | Clear does not mean safe

How many scientific questions can begin with one bottle of muddy water and a few filter materials? Designed for upper-elementary and early middle-school learners, this interactive lesson does not hand students a single “best” arrangement. Instead, they investigate settling, screening, and adsorption; design a fair test; compare clarity, flow rate, output, and cost; and improve their filter using evidence.

The lesson includes an atlas of ten filter materials, a configurable flow-and-layer simulator, repeat-trial recording tools, ten inquiry challenges, and six original Image 2.0 real-world scenes—from water treatment and rainwater reuse to coffee, aquariums, camping, and space-station water recovery.

The central safety message stays visible throughout: no simulated water sample may be consumed before or after filtering. Clear-looking water is an observation, not proof that it meets drinking-water standards. Invite your students to move from “it looks cleaner” to “the evidence shows…”
